01The Story
JABULANI
isiZulu — to rejoice.

ULANI is born from that word: an annual African cultural festival bringing music, food, fashion, beauty, art and ideas into one day. It honours those who came before us through history, creativity and joy, and imagines, boldly, what comes next.

"If you give people a taste of freedom, they will spend their lives chasing it." The founding belief of ULANI
